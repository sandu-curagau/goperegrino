GoPeregrino offline trail extract

Map tiles from OpenFreeMap (https://openfreemap.org/), based on OpenMapTiles
(https://www.openmaptiles.org/) and OpenStreetMap contributors
(https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright). The OSM-derived data is available
under the Open Database License (https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/).
This pack retains a subset of layers and fields near the four demo routes.
The generation script is scripts/gerar-mapa-offline.mjs.
